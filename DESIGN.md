# DESIGN.md — Larius Obsidian Private Wealth Terminal
## 1. Visual Direction & Domain Exemplars
Inspired by **Fey**, **Koyfin**, **Mercury Private Banking**, and **Linear**, combined with the user's monochrome executive references:
- **Philosophy**: Entrepreneurial discipline, zero visual noise, architectural precision, and high-density institutional clarity.
- **No AI Clichés**: Zero neon purple/cyan gradients, zero loud saturated orange blocks, zero developer debug tags (`<Component />`).

## 2. Color Palette (Phantom Black, Obsidian & Brushed Titanium)
- **Phantom Black (`#030304`)**: Primary viewport background canvas.
- **Deep Obsidian (`#09090B`)**: Primary card and sidebar surface.
- **Elevated Graphite (`#111114` / `#16161A`)**: Hover states, active segmented controls, table headers, and inner wells.
- **Charcoal Hairline (`#1E1E24` / `rgba(255,255,255,0.07)`)**: 1px structural borders with top specular highlight (`rgba(255,255,255,0.12)`).
- **Pure Platinum (`#F4F4F5` / `#FFFFFF`)**: High-contrast primary typography, active pills (white pill with `#09090B` text), and primary chart curve.
- **Brushed Titanium (`#A1A1AA`)**: Secondary labels, benchmark curves, and metallic bar gradients.
- **Muted Slate (`#71717A` / `#52525B`)**: Axis ticks, metadata, keyboard shortcuts.
- **Semantic Financial Signals Only**:
  - Positive / Alpha / Tax-Free: Muted Sage Emerald (`#34D399` text, `rgba(52,211,153,0.10)` badge).
  - Negative / Risk / Tax Drag: Muted Rose (`#F87171` text, `rgba(248,113,113,0.10)` badge).
  - Warm Champagne Silver (`#D4D4D8` / `#E5E5E5`): Subtle executive highlight.
